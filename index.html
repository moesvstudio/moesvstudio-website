About moesvstudio®

One studio, two screens

Moesvstudio® Inc. is an independent corporation, founded in 2026 by developer and storyteller Moe alBaghdadi. The studio works two sides of the same idea — stories you watch and games you play. Creating videos, Moesvstudio® tells true-crime stories visually. On iOS and Android, GhostRiders and Bloktopia turn that same visual-first instinct into games.
